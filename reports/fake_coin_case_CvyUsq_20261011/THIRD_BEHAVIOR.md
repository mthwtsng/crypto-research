# DgT6h: pre-graduation cohort and admission review

## Finding

The third frozen curve tape shows a repeated early-inventory relay shared with the preceding Cfiyy launch: four early buyers transferred their full observed buys to four different receiving wallets within about two seconds of the first creation receipt, and the four raw token quantities match Cfiyy exactly. Those receiving wallets then appeared as sell-side actors in DgT6; several sold in repeated 19,050,000,000,000-raw-token bites. Bounded funding records also link selected creator/holder wallets across the two launches.

This is strong evidence for a repeated acquisition-and-relay pattern and a recurring funding family. It does not establish a single beneficial owner or prove that the rising curve volume was fabricated. The repeated Cfiyy buyer cohort is much broader than the relay wallets, while overlap with the earlier Cvy cohort is small. A trading service, copy trading, inventory distribution, coordinated trading, and self-dealing remain distinguishable hypotheses.

## Entry chronology and the Watch decision

The first DgT6 receipt was observed at **23:51:42 UTC**. Its first verdict was `unknown:missing_holder_snapshot`; subsequent screens became `watch`. The screen copied into the S5 intent completed at **23:53:34.752**, and the ghost ledger recorded `OPEN` at **23:53:45.390**. It was 10.638 seconds old and still within the recorded holder-screen TTL. The entry record is unwhitelisted and identifies S5, but `actual_fill_established=false`, `our_signature=null`, and `placement_calibrated=false`. So the answer is **yes, the paper ledger admitted an entry with Watch; the frozen record does not show a live bot fill**. The saved policy explicitly allowed `watch_entry=allow_with_partial_coverage_logged` and required Unknown to skip; the readiness and entry checks accept an unexpired `clear` or `watch` verdict ([curve_detector.rs:601-614](../../src/bin/curve_detector.rs#L601), [curve_detector.rs:651-654](../../src/bin/curve_detector.rs#L651)).

At the tied screen, the bounded holder snapshot had 19 resolved owners and 164 successful receipt records. The verdict was `watch`, with zero launch-window funding records, zero cross-launch records, null retained-supply lower bound, no supporting wallets, and `weak_fingerprint_only` plus `cross_launch_probe_incomplete`. Cross-launch completeness was explicitly false. The screen report also says `primary_rate_limited=true`, sampled funding history for **8 of 19** owners, and includes the error “Funding history sampled for 8 of 19 current holder wallets (maximum 8).” Yet that row labels the report `coverage=complete`, `funding_probe_complete=true`, and `source_profiles_complete=true`; the source profile stage considers an empty candidate-source set complete ([holder_screen.rs:660-708](../../src/bot/holder_screen.rs#L660), [holder_screen.rs:942-981](../../src/bot/holder_screen.rs#L942)). The aggregate sampling limit is therefore not reflected in the detector’s funding-completeness bit. The explicit 8/19 error makes the limitation recoverable from the report, but it should not be represented downstream as full roster coverage.

At **23:54:26.609**, the later detector record changed to Unknown for `missing_mint_supply`; it did not report stale evidence. The detector’s general rule is that future-dated or stale required evidence takes precedence and yields Unknown, while incomplete relationship coverage otherwise prevents Clear ([fake_coin_detector.rs:913-975](../../src/bot/fake_coin_detector.rs#L913)). This case’s two Unknown causes are missing inputs, not an observed stale-evidence event.

## Curve path and early seller sequence

The receipt file has 308 signatures/observations spanning **23:51:42–23:54:27 UTC**. Through the S5 source transaction at slot 455839313, index 927, the bounded tape contains 224 non-creator events from 152 wallets: 174 buys and 50 sells; 22 wallets had both directions. Pump’s recorded real-SOL curve reserve rose from 0.296250054 SOL to a pre-entry high of 72.141858131 SOL at 23:52:26, fell as low as 53.519308383 SOL in the pre-entry sequence, and recovered to 72.018477517 SOL at the S5 source event. This is a rapid upward reserve path with a sizable pullback and recovery, roughly 12 SOL short of the 84-SOL graduation boundary. Reserve is a curve-state measure, not a price-return or PnL calculation.

The independent transfer review authenticates successful `TransferChecked` instructions and recipient owners from transaction token balances. In each launch, four first-burst buyer wallets transferred the full token quantity matching their frozen curve buy to four different wallets about two to three seconds after creation. The same four raw quantities recur across Cfiyy and DgT6, summing to 710,189,998,989,883 raw units (710.189998989883 million tokens, about 71.019% of nominal one-billion launch supply). The wallet identities differ between launches. See [TRANSFER_REVIEW.md](TRANSFER_REVIEW.md) and its source transaction/owner evidence in `early_inventory_transfers.json`.

For DgT6, the buyer-to-recipient pairs were `FySVY5… → BbiQH6…` (352,243,820,712,452 raw), `ikPSWAPS… → 7pVSNB…` (182,270,662,645,082), `DDPvEh… → B1fvn…` (106,467,965,872,131), and `2XUT95… → BMxDP…` (69,207,549,760,218). The first two larger receiving wallets sold without any DgT6 buy receipt in the frozen tape. By the S5 trigger:

| Receiving wallet | Matched initial transfer | Pre-trigger sell receipts | Entry-screen balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `BbiQH6…` | 352,243,820,712,452 | 19, totaling the same raw amount | No longer in the top-19 holder sample |
| `7pVSNB…` | 182,270,662,645,082 | 8 × 19,050,000,000,000 raw | 48,920,662,645,082 raw (4.8921%) |
| `B1fvn…` | 106,467,965,872,131 | 1 × 19,050,000,000,000 raw | 87,417,965,872,131 raw (8.7418%), exact arithmetic remainder |
| `BMxDP…` | 69,207,549,760,218 | None before entry | 69,207,549,760,218 raw (6.9208%) |

`BbiQH6…`’s 19 sell receipts include 18 exact 19.05-million-token bites and one smaller remainder; they span 23:51:58–23:53:16, with 99.5104 SOL of observed sell quote volume. `7pVSNB…` sold eight exact 19.05-million-token bites before entry, for 39.5000 SOL of observed sell quote volume. These are event-level gross quote amounts, not wallet profit. The 7pVS entry balance is 19.05 million tokens above the arithmetic remainder from the one authenticated initial transfer and the eight saved sells. The bounded relay list does not reconcile that residual; additional token movements or inventory are unobserved here. `B1fvn…`’s one saved sell leaves exactly its entry-screen balance, while `BMxDP…` had not sold by the entry screen. This local balance check supports a transfer-to-seller path for two wallets and also shows why the four transfer rows cannot be treated as a complete inventory ledger.

At entry, the top three resolved holders were `B1fvn…`, `BMxDP…`, and `7pVSNB…`, retaining 20.5546% together; all 19 sampled holders retained 34.0513% in total. The first three therefore cross the policy’s 20% percentage by themselves, but no single narrow launch-window funder linked all three. The historic source-family transfers below were almost two hours before launch and outside the configured five-minute prelaunch/30-second postlaunch funding window. Retained supply alone is not the detector’s hard-block condition.

## Repeated cohort and funding links

At the DgT6 entry cutoff, **112 of 152** observed non-creator wallets had also traded in the preceding Cfiyy tape (73.7% of DgT6 pre-entry wallets; 47.9% of Cfiyy’s 234 observed external wallets). The prior-to-first-DgT receipt gaps for these wallets span about 13m10s–16m48s, all outside the detector’s 10-minute exact-address history. Of the 112, 99 had only a buy in each observed tape by entry; 13 had at least one DgT6 sell by entry. Across the full 23:54:27 DgT6 snapshot, the exact Cfiyy overlap grows to **143 of 199** external DgT6 wallets; later events account for the additional identities. The entry-time number, rather than this later total, is the relevant one for admission.

Fifteen of the 19 DgT6 entry-screen holder addresses occur in the Cfiyy receipt cohort, together retaining 12.3660% of supply. The repeat-user signal is broad and mostly buy-only; it is association evidence, not proof those addresses share an owner. By comparison, the Cvy receipt cohort has only five exact address overlaps with the full DgT6 tape and four present by the S5 entry cutoff. The strongest observed repeat is therefore specifically between the adjacent Cfiyy and DgT6 cases, not a large three-case wallet intersection.

The separately saved bounded funding sample adds three cross-launch source families: `5tzFki…` sent 0.065732 SOL in the same signature to Cfiyy holder `GV1Pw…` and DgT6 holder `B1fvn…`; `BmFdp…` sent the same 0.057667-SOL seed amount to Cfiyy `3rro…` and DgT6 `BbiQH6…`; and `iGdFc…` sent transfers to Cfiyy `DhrD…` and DgT6 creator `Fabbi…`, `BMxDP…`, and `7pVSNB…`. These selected links are about 1h53–1h56 before the DgT6 launch. The selected-wallet manifest resolves 48/48 requested transactions but marks funding history incomplete. A separate bounded 50-signature-per-source sample observed 28, 53, and 34 outgoing recipients for these sources, respectively; it does not prove complete source degree. This supports a recurring funding family, while the high observed recipient counts make service/platform funding a material alternative to a narrow owner-controlled funder. No link establishes ownership.

The detector’s current wiring captures curve buyers into a rolling exact-address history, expires that history after 600 seconds, and caps each mint’s relationship receipt ring at 256. It only proposes a cross-launch cohort after at least three linked holders overlap, then unconditionally supplies `cross_launch_probe_complete=false` ([curve_detector.rs:297-335](../../src/bin/curve_detector.rs#L297), [curve_detector.rs:387-410](../../src/bin/curve_detector.rs#L387)). The entry screen’s 164 receipts were below the 256 ring cap, so this case did not lose its own receipts to that cap at entry; the later DgT6 tape passes 256 and would become incomplete. The time window is the direct reason the prior Cfiyy cohort was unavailable at entry.

More fundamentally, this relay begins with **token transfers**, not SOL funding transfers. The holder screen’s funding parser accepts successful parsed System Program funding instructions; the fake-coin input builder then consumes those incoming-funding edges. It does not connect the transfer recipients to the original curve buyers through SPL token transfers ([holder_screen.rs:1751-1789](../../src/bot/holder_screen.rs#L1751), [curve_detector.rs:356-365](../../src/bin/curve_detector.rs#L356)). The current hard-block rule also requires one verified narrow launch funder, at least three receipt-linked recipients, a retained lower bound of at least 20%, and an independent corroborator; coverage gaps alone produce Watch rather than Block ([fake_coin_detector.rs:655-710](../../src/bot/fake_coin_detector.rs#L655), [fake_coin_detector.rs:765-870](../../src/bot/fake_coin_detector.rs#L765)). With no launch-window funding candidates and no available cross-launch history at entry, this evidence did not satisfy that conjunction.

## Evidence-backed follow-up, without threshold changes

1. Add an auditable token-inventory path feature: verified buy → successful SPL transfer with owner attribution → recipient sell receipts, with balance reconciliation and protocol/vault accounts excluded. Require exact owner and mint matches; do not infer a relay from a common amount or generic transaction structure alone.
2. Persist exact-address cross-launch co-buys beyond ten minutes and across restarts, while recording the time horizon and event-age coverage. Treat a repeated cohort as review evidence until larger ordinary-launch comparisons establish its false-positive rate.
3. Split funding coverage into “per sampled wallet probe succeeded” and “all eligible holder wallets sampled.” The 8/19 entry screen should not expose a complete-funding flag. Keep source-profile completeness distinct from an empty candidate set.
4. Evaluate recurring high-degree funders and two- or three-wallet retained concentrations as separate review features; preserve service, exchange, relayer, arbitrage and market-maker controls. Do not infer narrow common ownership from the current 20% threshold or lower the minimum-wallet rule on this case.

The records establish a repeated inventory-relay template, a large exact Cfiyy/DgT6 trader overlap, and selected common-funder associations. They do not establish fake volume, shared beneficial ownership, or a rug. No source, policy, runtime, or order settings were changed. No RPC was run for this report; no backtest or PnL claim is made.
