By the S1 entry cutoff, the 17 non-creator GKGnA wallets had **zero** address matches to all four prior receipt sets. The nine entry-screen holder addresses likewise had zero matches. This is consistent with no visible prior-cohort identity among this coin’s initial buyer/holder slice; it does not prove the broader cohort could not have supplied inventory through unobserved transfers.

The four previously noted paired Cvy wallets—`B9ksm…` / `CMcK…` and `Hy4Zur…` / `7dGV…`—have no receipt event in the 214-row GKGnA tape. Their highly synchronized prior events may justify automation/copy-trading review, but they do not implicate this mint. Conversely, the 31-wallet GKGnA sell burst is a separate synchronized-timing feature. Publicly visible reserve collapse can trigger independent arbitrage, liquidation bots, and copy sellers; near-coincident sells alone cannot distinguish these from common control or wash activity.
