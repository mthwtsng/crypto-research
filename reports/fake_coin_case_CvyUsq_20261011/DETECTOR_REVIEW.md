## What the block rule actually asks for

The current policy is a narrow retained-ownership screen. Its logged settings here are at least **3 wallets**, at least **20% of actual mint supply** retained by the candidate cluster in the bounded current-holder sample, a launch-proximate direct funder whose complete bounded-window recipient degree is at most **8**, and corroboration: that same funder pays at least three distinct buyer receipts; or a repeated buyer set contains at least three buyers across at least two signatures; or a verified cross-launch cohort overlaps at least three candidate wallets. The launch funding window is five minutes before through 30 seconds after creation; relationship evidence expires after 10 minutes and the holder snapshot after 30 seconds. Sources: `src/bin/curve_detector.rs:58-75`; `src/bot/fake_coin_detector.rs:207-235`, `399-435`, `655-710`, `765-870`.

Funding is especially scoped: the holder screen samples up to the 20 largest token accounts and resolves eligible wallet owners, then probes only the first eight resolved wallets. Per selected wallet, it requests at most 10 signatures and inspects at most two eligible transactions. Source profiling considers at most two funding sources with at least three sampled recipients and scans at most 16 source signatures. It retains successful, parsed System Program incoming transfers in the launch window while excluding zero-value, self-funded and non-wallet/program sources. The detector consumes only records marked `verified_system_transfer`; parsed `createAccount` instructions are collected but do not pass that transfer predicate. Sources: `src/bot/holder_screen.rs:21-40`, `520-563`, `660-715`, `942-981`, `1257-1325`, `1556-1661`, `1750-1789`; `src/bin/curve_detector.rs:356-365`.

The four entry-time funding records are four distinct sources, one sampled destination each. The logged `funder_scope_unverified` flag alone does **not** mean there was a three-wallet shared-funder candidate: the detector emits that reason for any valid source lacking a verified narrow profile; it marks an unverified candidate only when one source reaches at least three linked wallets. Here no such common source was established in the emitted sample. The holder screen profiles only sources reaching at least three sampled recipients (up to two sources); with no candidate, `source_profiles_complete=true` is vacuous and does not certify these four sources as narrow. The receipt data also does not show a current retained-supply cluster: the verdict has no supporting wallets and leaves the retained-supply percentage unset. The blocker was not defeated by a measured cluster falling below 20%; it had no evaluated qualifying cluster to score. Sources: `src/bot/fake_coin_detector.rs:655-701`; `src/bot/holder_screen.rs:942-981`.

The live code builds cross-launch candidates from exact-address overlaps among successful SOL-quoted buys in its in-memory rolling history, but passes `cross_launch_probe_complete=false` unconditionally. ## Gap assessment and competing explanations

**Could a real coordinated wash group avoid `blocked` because it retains less than 20%? Yes, by design.** If linked wallets cycle through buys and sells, spread balances across more wallets, use separate or non-transfer funding paths, or fall outside the sampled/current set, the hard block can fail even when upward volume is coordinated. Since this policy allows `watch` entries, partial coverage or weak-only risk indicators do not themselves stop a paper entry. This is a detector-scope gap if the goal is to screen wash volume; it is not evidence that this mint was washed. The 20% threshold is explicitly labeled an initial heuristic, not a validated calibration.

The frozen entry-time detector evidence supports these narrower conclusions: a recent Watch verdict was admitted; local receipt coverage had saturated its cap; cross-launch coverage was explicitly incomplete; and the sampled direct funding records did not form a corroborated narrow-funder retained-supply cluster. It does **not** decide whether the repeated buyer behavior described by the user reflects shared control, legitimate momentum participants, a copier cohort, market making, or another mechanism. The separate receipt/volume interpretation belongs to the broader event-level review.

## Evidence-supported changes to consider

1. **Separate admission, risk and coverage states.** Make Watch reasons explicit: behavior-risk Watch and incomplete-coverage Watch should not be one state with one automatic admission behavior. If the goal is to avoid uncertain coins, hold the relevant Watch class for a fresh recheck; count the likely false skips caused by rate limits and probe gaps. Keep historical funding and buyer relationships with their timestamps and source, while marking them `stale_for_current_verdict`; refresh holder state independently. Preserve fail-closed admission for Unknown, but name the evidence that aged out. This keeps old funding facts visible without treating them as current proof. A fresh, independently corroborated retained-balance snapshot should remain necessary for a hard block.
2. **Make sampled funding coverage explicit and consistent.** Emit separate counts for resolved holder wallets, funding-probed wallets and coverage scope. Either call 8/19 `sampled_partial` or require the selected count to equal the intended universe before setting full-scope coverage. Keep partial coverage as Watch/abstention; never convert unprobed wallets into “no funding link.” The entry log demonstrates this reporting mismatch directly.
3. **Repair and persist cross-launch coverage before relying on it.** The current collector is a short rolling in-memory observation and always labels the probe incomplete. If recurring buyers are to count, retain receipt provenance across restarts and expose observed-window coverage, unique launches, timestamps and current wallet overlap. Do not turn co-buy recurrence alone into a hard block; repeated independent momentum buying is a plausible false positive.
4. **Add a distinct wash-pattern evidence layer if that is the desired policy.** The present receipt ring retains buys, payer, fingerprint and buyer sets, but no matched per-wallet buy/sell inventory cycles. A candidate signal should use complete successful receipts, same-wallet net token changes, round-trip timing, repeated pattern counts across launches and the share of volume that reverses, with fee payer/funding as corroboration. Treat this as review/Watch evidence until independently supported. Control false positives from market makers, arbitrage, common relayers and momentum copiers; do not infer coordination from rising price, volume, low SOL balance or repeated co-buys alone.
5. **Keep the retained-supply threshold provisional.** This case provides no calibration for lowering 20%. Compare any alternative on independently reviewed coordinated and ordinary cohorts, and report miss and false-block rates before changing it. Retained balance can support a concentration risk rule, but low current inventory cannot establish benign behavior.
