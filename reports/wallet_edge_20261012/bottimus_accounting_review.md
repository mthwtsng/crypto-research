# Bottimus group-accounting review

This is a bounded receipt and execution-profile review of `bcnzSs3F6F3MG7utvEQHpd8A53nRBM8ZM4TfEs2Zmcp` and its authenticated trading children. I read `accounting.py` without changing it. The group collector and output are `bottimus_accounting.py` and `bottimus_complete.json`.

The source is 10,000 verified full transaction receipts across 10 pages, from 2026-10-07 10:47:38 UTC through 2026-10-12 04:10:43 UTC (4.724 days). The requested range is 21 days, but the collection reports `truncated_page_cap`; it is not lifetime or full-window coverage. All 12 role-listed child addresses have signed authenticated trade events and at least one successful SSS link receipt or direct parent funding receipt. Their direct-funding and SSS evidence is repeated, not a single-address inference. That supports treating them as a controller-funded execution group for cash reconciliation; it does not establish common beneficial ownership.

Across the group, the parser found 8,490 authenticated Pump/PumpSwap event occurrences. The stricter cycle matcher retained 6,010 receipts where there is exactly one changed group `(event owner, mint)`, the sum of that owner's event token deltas exactly matches the pre/post token-balance delta, and every event is SOL-quoted. It leaves 2,937 token-changing receipts unclassified, 4 exact trade receipts inherited/no-zero-inventory openings, and 248 positive owner/mint inventories observed at the end of the receipt window. The reported zero active cycles therefore does not mean the wallets have no open inventory. The 2,755 complete cycles are bounded observed owner/mint zero-to-zero sequences, not a copy-trading replay or a PnL verdict.

Cash per matched transaction sums native balance changes for the payer and all 12 linked children, then adds their WSOL token-balance change. Parent-to-child and child-to-parent transfers therefore cancel in the group total. Network fees paid by a group member, explicit SOL tips, and account funding/refunds remain in the observed balance changes; successful fees are not subtracted a second time. Failed receipts are excluded from trade cycles; the 526 failed attempts paid by a group payer cost 0.962400049 SOL across the full receipt window. That whole-window fee total should remain separate from cycle cash rather than be read as a fee-adjusted cycle result.

Standalone transfers cannot open or close a cycle because a cycle leg also requires a decoded trade event and an exact token-balance delta. In the three reviewed cycles, the direct SOL legs were the source trade/tip routes and the repeated sweeps between child and payer; group aggregation cancels the sweeps. The matcher does not separately attribute an unrelated transfer if it is co-packed into an otherwise matched swap transaction. Such a transfer would remain in the transaction's cash delta, so mixed-purpose same-transaction flows are a remaining attribution limit. A full all-receipt System-transfer scan found no parsed outside-to-group System transfer among the 6,010 matched trade receipts; this does not rule out program-mediated cash flows or non-System transfer forms.

Rent figures are diagnostics, not expenses: `system_account_create_lamports` records parsed System account creations funded by group members, while `closed_token_account_lamports` is the full lamport balance returned by parsed token-account closes. The latter can contain WSOL principal as well as rent, and not every close/create pair is necessarily attributable to the trade mint. The observed group cash already contains the actual debit and refund. The report also records zero-token-account lamports at exit where metadata permits; no unrecovered-rent writeoff is fabricated.

## Accounting limits for follow-up

- The linked group is a high-confidence execution/custody association, not a claim about unified beneficial ownership. Per-owner/mint inventory is kept separate; group balances are summed only so internal SOL transfers do not masquerade as proceeds or costs.
- Token transfers, non-SOL quote trades, mixed-owner/mint receipts, and other nonmatching token changes remain unclassified and taint an active cycle when they change that active owner/mint. A source position already nonzero at the first observed eligible trade is retained as inherited rather than assigned a fabricated entry.
- The 248 observed positive end-window inventories are explicitly retained with last-seen receipt provenance. No mark or migration value is inferred from them.
- Fees from 526 failed group-payer attempts are window-level evidence. They are not assigned to the 2,755 completed cycles, and should not be netted against them as though both populations were the same cohort.
- Historical event quotes and cycle durations describe the source bot's observed activity. They do not show what a copier would have received, whether these cash deltas are replicable, or why another strategy underperforms.
